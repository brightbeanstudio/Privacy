Privacy Policy – 2048 Merge Puzzle

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how the game "2048 Merge Puzzle" (the "App"), made by Bright Bean Studio, handles information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your scores, saved games and settings (sound, theme) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app usage information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Users in the European Economic Area and UK are asked for consent and can change it anytime from the App's Settings → "Privacy & ad choices".

3. Online leaderboard

If you use the world leaderboard, the nickname you choose, your best score, biggest tile and a random player ID are sent to Google Firebase and shown to other players. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown. This data is not linked to your identity. To delete your entry, email us.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is only shared with the service providers above (Google AdMob, Google Firebase) to run the App.
